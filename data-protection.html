1. Our approach

FH Development follows UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We collect only the personal data we need, keep it accurate, and keep it only as long as necessary.

2. Lawful bases

We process data to perform our contract with you (delivering your purchase), to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and providing support.

3. Security

Access to customer data is limited to people who need it, and we use reputable providers with appropriate security measures. Payment card details are handled by Payhip and its payment partners, not stored by us.

4. Data breaches

If a breach puts people’s rights at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours where required and tell affected people without undue delay.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as approved contractual clauses.

6. Requests and contact

To exercise your rights or ask a question, email support@fh-development.xyz. We aim to respond within one month.
