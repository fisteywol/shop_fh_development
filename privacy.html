1. What this covers

This policy explains how FH Development handles personal data when you use this store.

2. What we collect

Orders and payments are handled by Payhip, which collects the details needed to complete your purchase (such as your name, email address and payment details). We receive order information from Payhip, such as your email address and what you bought. If you email support, we keep that correspondence.

3. How we use it

We use your data to deliver products, provide support, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the store (for example Payhip, our hosting provider and our email provider). We do not sell personal data.

5. Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. Payhip may set cookies for checkout and the cart; see Payhip’s privacy policy.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Email support@fh-development.xyz. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Retention

We keep order and support records only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.
